# Changelog

One entry for each merged piece, newest first. The gate writes each entry from the
piece's behaviour change, never from a commit message. An entry sits under the
day the piece reached `main`, under Added, Changed, Removed, Fixed or Security,
and ends with the piece link, written `[piece <number>](<link>)`.
