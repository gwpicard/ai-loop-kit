/shape let a steward add a short note to a loan when it comes back, such as "lens cap missing", and show the latest note on the item's page
---
# when: \?
Only stewards write the note, when they mark the loan returned or later. One note per loan, at most 200 characters. A longer one is refused with a message that says the limit.
---
# when: \?
A steward can change or clear the note, and there is no undo beyond that. The note goes when the loan goes. Nothing about it goes to the calendar.
---
